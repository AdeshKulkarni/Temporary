5. Voucher Authorization Workflow
(Roles: TCS GLIF Checkers, SBI GLIF Approvers)
Once a Maker has successfully prepared and submitted a voucher batch, it moves out of their hands and enters the authorization pipeline. This module explains how Checkers and Approvers review the data, verify its accuracy, and make their final decisions.
5.1 The Workflow Explained
To ensure financial security, the system uses a strict Maker-Checker process. A batch cannot be posted to the ledger by the same person who created it. It must travel through a designated approval path.
1. Maker: Creates and submits the transaction data.
2. Checker(s): Acts as the first line of defense to review the numbers and approve the batch.
3. Final Approver: Provides the ultimate sign-off before the money actually moves.
4. System: Automatically executes the approved batch into the core ledger.
5.2 Reviewing Pending Requests
To see the work that requires your attention, navigate to the Voucher Authorization screen from the main menu.
Think of this screen as your personal inbox. You will see a table listing all the voucher batches that are currently paused and waiting for your specific role to approve them. If a batch is assigned to a different team, it will not clutter your screen.
5.3 Using the "View & Action" Button
You cannot approve a batch blindly. To see the details of a request, locate it in your table and click the View & Action button. This will open a detailed summary screen.
5.3.1 Reviewing Batch and Voucher Details
At the very top of the screen, you are given the complete context of the request. You can see the Voucher ID (VID), the business justification (Description), the category, who created it, the total number of transaction entries, and the exact date and time it was submitted.
5.3.2 Downloading the Data Extract
If the batch contains dozens or hundreds of rows, you will want to inspect the exact financial entries. Simply click the Download Batch button. The system will instantly download an Excel file containing every single row, amount, and remark inside that batch for you to review offline.
5.3.3 Tracking Workflow Progress & History
As you scroll down, you will see a visual timeline. This stepper shows you exactly where the batch currently is, which stages are completed, and which are still pending.
Below the timeline is the Workflow History. This is your audit trail. It tells you exactly who handled the batch before you, what time they approved it, and any comments they left for you to read.
5.4 Executing an Authorization Decision
Once you are satisfied with your review, scroll to the bottom of the screen and click the Proceed to Action button to record your decision.
5.4.1 Approving a Request
If the data is correct, click the green Approve Request button. This will successfully pass the batch forward to the next person in the chain.
5.4.2 Rejecting a Request
If you spot an error, click the red Reject Request button. This immediately terminates the workflow. The batch will not move forward, and the Maker will be notified that their batch was denied.
5.4.3 Providing Action Remarks (Mandatory)
Whether you are approving or rejecting, you cannot submit your decision in silence. You must type a reason or comment in the Action Remarks box.
￼ Rule to remember: Your remark must be between 2 and 30 characters long. To keep the database clean, please only use letters, numbers, and a single space between words.
5.5 Final Approver Privileges & Schedule Overrides
If you are the "Final Approver" at the very end of the workflow, you are granted special scheduling privileges. Before you click submit, you must tell the system when to execute the batch:
￼ Keep Original Routing: Select this to honor the schedule originally chosen by the Maker.
￼ Override Schedule: Select this if you want to pick a brand-new execution time for today. (Note: If the Maker scheduled the batch for 2:00 PM, but you don't approve it until 3:00 PM, the original time has lapsed. The system will force you to use this override option to pick a new future time).
6. Voucher Posting Status & Tracking
(Roles: All users involved in the voucher flow)
While the Authorization screen is an inbox for taking action, the Voucher Posting Status screen is a tracking dashboard. Anyone involved in a batch's lifecycle can come here to monitor its real-time progress.
6.1 Searching and Filtering Batches
If you are looking for a specific transaction, use the search boxes located at the top of the table columns. You can quickly filter the list by typing in the exact Batch ID or the VID.
6.2 Understanding Batch Statuses
The "Workflow Progress" column gives you a clear visual indicator of what is happening with the batch right now:
￼ Pending: The batch is currently paused and waiting for a specific person to approve it.
￼ Completed / Posted: Success! The batch was fully approved and the system has successfully posted the money to the ledger.
￼ Rejected: An approver found an error and denied the batch. The process has been stopped entirely.
￼ Escalated: The batch has been sitting in someone's pending queue for too long without them taking action, triggering a warning.
￼ Execution Failed: The batch was fully approved, but the system encountered a technical server error while trying to post it. (Don't worry, the Production Support Team is automatically notified to safely retry it).
6.3 Cancelling a Draft/Submitted Batch (Maker Only)
If you are the Maker who created a batch and you realize you made a mistake after hitting submit, you might be able to recall it.
You can permanently delete a batch only if it is still sitting at the very first pending stage (meaning the first Checker hasn't approved it yet).
To do this, click View & Action on your batch, scroll to the very bottom of the details window, and click the red Cancel Voucher Batch button. This will destroy the batch and unlock your VID so you can start over.
