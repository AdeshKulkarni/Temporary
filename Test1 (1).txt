File Naming Instructions
To complete your CI/CD setup, you must create 5 separate files in your src/main/resources folder using the exact naming convention your pipeline expects.
Paste the code block above into every single one of these files:
 * application-dev.properties
 * application-st.properties
 * application-uat.properties
 * application-preprod.properties
 * application-prod.properties
(You can also keep a base application.properties with just spring.profiles.active=dev for when you run it locally on your own machine).
During deployment, your CI/CD pipeline will set the environment variables (like REDIS_HOST or JWT_SECRET) securely in the background, and Spring Boot will automatically inject them into the corresponding properties file.
