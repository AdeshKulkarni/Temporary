# Problem: Omnichannel AI Support Dispatcher

**Difficulty:** MEDIUM/HARD
**Patterns Required:** FACTORY METHOD + STRATEGY
**Real Company Example:** Intercom, Zendesk, Twilio

**The Scenario:**
You are building the core routing engine for a modern customer support platform. Your system receives customer complaints from three different platforms: a Phone Hotline, WhatsApp, and an old Legacy Website Chat.

**Current Pain:**
Right now, your codebase has a giant `CustomerServiceRouter` class. Every time a message comes in, there are massive nested `if-else` blocks checking the platform type to figure out how to read the message, how to judge the customer's mood, and what to do if the bot fails.

**The Catch:**
Creating a "Support Bot" to handle a message requires configuring three distinct behavioral rules. These rules are heavily mixed and matched depending on the platform.

### Business Requirements (Design the Architecture!)

**1. The Reusable Behaviors (The Rules):**
You must design a way to plug these specific rules into the bots without hardcoding them into the bot classes. 
*   **Message Processing (NLP):**
    *   *LLM Agent:* Uses advanced ChatGPT AI to understand complex phrasing.
    *   *Intent Matcher:* Uses standard Google Dialogflow to match sentences to FAQ articles.
    *   *Keyword Regex:* Looks for exact words like "refund" or "broken" (very basic).
*   **Sentiment Analysis (Mood Tracking):**
    *   *Voice Tone:* Analyzes the pitch and volume of an audio file to see if the customer is yelling.
    *   *Text Sentiment:* Scans text for angry words or exclamation marks.
*   **Human Handoff (Escalation):**
    *   *Live Transfer:* Instantly connects the user to an active agent's chat screen.
    *   *Create Ticket:* Logs a low-priority ticket in the database for an agent to answer later.
    *   *Call Routing:* Puts the user on hold and plays elevator music until a human picks up.

**2. Platform Setup Rules (The Assembly phase):**
Your system needs a clean way to spawn fully configured Bots based on where the message came from. 
*   **Phone Bot:** Needs to understand speech (`LLM Agent`), track yelling (`Voice Tone`), and route to a live human if things go wrong (`Call Routing`).
*   **WhatsApp Bot:** Needs standard text matching (`Intent Matcher`), text mood tracking (`Text Sentiment`), and instant live chat transfers (`Live Transfer`).
*   **Legacy Web Bot:** Uses basic word searches (`Keyword Regex`), text mood tracking (`Text Sentiment`), and just logs tickets if it fails (`Create Ticket`).

**3. The End Goal (The Product):**
The main application (your `Main` method) should be completely blind to how the bots work. It just wants to ask for a bot (e.g., "Give me the WhatsApp Bot"), hand it a customer message string, and tell it to `handleCustomer()`.
