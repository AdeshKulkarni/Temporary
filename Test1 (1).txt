1.1 Purpose of this Document
This user manual is your complete guide to using the Voucher Posting & Authorization System. It is written to help everyone involved in the financial pipeline—whether you are a Maker preparing the data, a Checker reviewing it, or a Final Approver authorizing the transaction.
This guide will walk you through everything you need to know, from setting up a new Voucher ID (VID) to submitting transactions (either manually or using an Excel upload) and navigating the approval process. Our goal is to ensure you can confidently submit, review, and track your financial batches while easily understanding the rules that keep our ledger secure.
1.2 System Overview & Key Features
The Voucher Posting & Authorization System is designed to make financial entries as secure and error-free as possible. It automatically checks your data for mistakes before submission and requires a multi-step approval process before any money actually moves in the core ledger.
Key Features Include:
￼ Maker-Checker Security: To prevent errors or fraud, the person who creates a batch of transactions can never be the same person who approves it.
￼ Voucher IDs (VIDs): Think of a VID as a pre-approved folder. You cannot just post random transactions; every transaction must be placed inside an approved VID folder to ensure it is authorized by management.
￼ Flexible Data Entry: You can quickly type a few adjustments directly into the system, or you can upload thousands of rows at once using a standard Excel template.
￼ Built-in Balance Checks: The system will not let you submit a batch unless your total Debits and total Credits match perfectly.
￼ Execution Scheduling: Once a batch is fully approved, it doesn't have to post immediately. You can schedule it to hit the ledger later in the day, avoiding busy hours.
￼ Clear Audit Trails: You can always see exactly where a batch is, who has approved it, and what comments they left along the way.

2. Getting Started
2.1 System Access and Roles
What you can see and do in the system depends entirely on your assigned job role.
A. Maker Roles (e.g., TCS GLIF & SBI GLIF)
If you are assigned a Maker role, you are the starting point of the process. Your main responsibilities include:
￼ Voucher Posting: Creating new transaction batches (either by typing them in or uploading an Excel file).
￼ Tracking Status: Checking the progress of the batches you submitted to see who is currently reviewing them.
￼ Reviewing Others: Sometimes acting as a first-level Checker to review batches submitted by your teammates.
￼ Using Inquiry Screens: Searching through the system to double-check Branch Codes, General Ledgers (CGL), and specific voucher categories.
B. Admin & Final Approver Roles (e.g., SBI GLIF)
If you are an Admin or Final Approver, you manage the system's rules and provide the final sign-off.
￼ Managing VIDs: Creating the "Voucher IDs" that the Makers need before they can start working. You can also view or close active VIDs.
￼ Final Authorization: Reviewing fully verified batches and giving the final approval.
￼ Setting the Schedule: Deciding if an approved batch should post to the ledger right now or at a scheduled time later today.
2.2 Important Business Rules & Constraints
To protect the integrity of our financial data, the system strictly enforces a few non-negotiable rules. Please keep these in mind so your batches are not rejected:
1. Perfect Balancing: The system will not let you move forward unless your Total Debits equal your Total Credits. It checks this balance specifically for each Branch and Currency combination.
2. No Self-Approval: You cannot approve your own work. If you submit a batch, the "Approve" button will simply be disabled for you when you look at that batch in the Authorization screen.
3. Amount Rules: You cannot enter an amount of zero, and you cannot enter negative numbers. If you need to reverse an amount, you must enter a positive number on the opposite side (e.g., use a Credit instead of a Debit). Amounts can have up to 4 decimal places.
4. Clean Remarks: Whenever you leave a comment or a remark, it must be between 2 and 30 characters long. Please only use regular letters, numbers, and single spaces. The system will block special characters to keep the database clean.
3. Voucher ID (VID) Creation
3.1 Understanding the Role of the VID
What is a VID?
A VID (Voucher ID) is essentially a digital permission slip. It looks something like ⁠VID-2409-12345⁠.
Why do we need it?
In the past, users might post adjustments freely. Now, a supervisor or admin must create a VID first. This ensures that every single financial entry is tied to a specific, pre-approved business reason. It also gives management an easy way to track all the money moved for a specific project, or quickly close a VID to stop any more entries from being posted to it.
3.2 Creating a New VID
If you have the Admin role, you are responsible for creating these VIDs for your team.
To create one, you simply fill out the "Request Voucher" form. You will be asked to explain the business reason for the VID. If this voucher is tied to a ticket from an external system (like the SCORE system), you can optionally type that ticket number in the "SD Request Number" box to link the two systems together.
Once you click "Create," the system generates the unique VID number instantly. Your Makers can now select this VID and begin uploading their financial data against it.
