Here are the 3 exact reasons why it was failing, and how we are going to fix them:
 * The Disappearing Bug: VoucherQueryService has a rule: "If a user has acted on a batch, hide it from their queue." Because they just clicked Approve, the system hides it! We will add an exception: "Hide it... UNLESS the latest action is 'Execution Failed'."
 * The "Already Approved" Bug: If they try to click Retry, the backend VoucherWorkflowEngine blocks them and says "You have already approved this batch!". We need to tell the engine to bypass this rule during a Retry.
 * The UI Button: We will pull the Retry button completely out of the modal and put it directly on the Table grid!

      setIsSubmitting(false);
    }
  };
